04 — About

A little about me.

Paulina is a senior at The New School pursuing her B.A. in Literary Studies with a concentration in Literature and Critical Analysis.

Born and raised in Puerto Rico, her culture and identity informs her work and the perspective she brings to literary criticism and theory.

Inspired by authors like bell hooks, Kaveh Akbar, Julia de Burgos, and James Baldwin, Paulina hopes to continue her academic and professional career through the world of books. Particularly interested in the publishing industry, she hopes to contribute to the process of bringing meaningful literary works to readers.
